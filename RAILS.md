# Every remaining way money could arrive — honest edition

Ranked by fit with the constraints: **zero capital, zero AI (from 2026-09-14), human only for
identity/payout clicks**. The playbook's field study is the prior here: most rails measured $0.
This page is the standing menu; nothing on it costs money.

  automated age+stars honeypot gate) for fresh unclaimed reproducible bugs. Email alarm fires on

  bounties are announced in STATUS.md when they appear (today's was capital-gated).

## 2. The only true "money for nothing": bandwidth sharing (~$20–50/mo total)

Apps pay you to route ordinary web traffic through your connection. Real 2026 numbers: $5–25/mo
per app; people stack several. **Tradeoffs you accept**: strangers' traffic exits via your IP
(abuse lands on your connection), some ISP terms prohibit it, payouts need an account
(PayPal/crypto), and it is pennies, not income. Setup is 20 minutes of clicks in a browser — no
AI involved. Apps: [Honeygain](https://www.honeygain.com/), [Pawns.app](https://pawns.app/),
[EarnApp](https://earnapp.com/). Never install on a work/school network.

## 3. GitHub Sponsors (5 min once, then passive)

The repo already advertises it via `.github/FUNDING.yml`. To actually receive: enable your
Sponsors profile at https://github.com/sponsors (Stripe identity check once). Realistic odds
without an audience: near zero, but nothing to maintain.

## 4. The big prizes need the human (honest note)

The largest live bounties found this session were **content/creator** ones (e.g. $10,000 USDG
Solana Summit creator challenge, $500–7,000 video bounties) — all marked HUMAN_ONLY because they
need your face/voice. If you ever want one, it's the highest-$ per hour of *your* time available
in this ecosystem, and an AI session can do the production work around you.

## 5. What was evaluated and rejected (so you don't wonder)

- **Paid auctions / token sales** (Umia): costs money.
- **Discount-for-data programs** (dFusion AI): pays discounts on purchases, not money.
- **Engagement/token-farming "bounties"** (upvote-for-RTC etc.): worthless tokens.
- **Bounty boards < 180 days old**: honeypots (see LOG 2026-09-13).
- **AI-training gig platforms**: bans *your* identity when they detect agent labor.
- **Multi-wallet airdrop farming**: sybil detection burns the wallets; one honest wallet each.
- **x402 paid APIs / agent job boards**: measured at $0.00 demand by the field study.
